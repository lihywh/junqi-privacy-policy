Privacy policy

Effective date · 2026-11-15

The app and its operator

Junqi Table (com.amao.junqi) is operated by 李文环, publishing as lihywh. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to lihywh@gmail.com. The game does not require an account and has no developer-operated matchmaking or cloud-game server.

Local play and storage

AI play and match recaps are processed on the device. Training progress, saved formations and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Current-game replay and recaps use information available to the player. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or payment SDK.

Nearby matches

Only when you choose a nearby match does the app use the local network or Bluetooth. It exchanges game rules, deployments, moves, public events, board snapshots and temporary session credentials with the device you connect to. The host device processes both deployments to judge the game; the guest receives its permitted view of the board. Network addresses, Bluetooth device names and device addresses are handled for discovery, pairing and connection, not advertising or tracking. Game traffic uses an encrypted connection. Compare the six-digit code on both screens before confirming the opponent.

Permissions

Network and nearby-device permissions enable Wi-Fi discovery and Bluetooth connections. Older Android versions may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery; the app does not request GPS fixes or build a physical location history. Newer systems may ask for local-network access. The game does not request access to your contacts, call logs, photos, microphone or camera. Bluetooth pairing is managed by Android; the game does not need the optional contacts or call-history sharing that a system pairing screen may offer.

Recipients and retention

Nearby game information is sent to the opponent device selected by you. There is no game-data upload to an advertising service, analytics service or developer game server. Nearby session and replay data are held in memory until replaced or the app process ends; a temporary disconnection may keep the session for recovery. Saved training and settings remain until you replace them, clear app storage or uninstall. Android may retain Bluetooth pairing information until you unpair the devices.

Deletion, backup and your choices

You can play against AI without granting nearby permissions, revoke permissions in Android settings, disconnect from a match, and clear local data using Android’s app-storage controls. Uninstalling removes app-private data according to Android behavior. The app requests exclusion from Android cloud backup and device transfer; OS or manufacturer migration settings may still affect copies outside this app’s control. Deleting one device’s data does not delete another player’s data or a system migration copy.

Support and external pages

If you email support, we process the return address and the content you choose to send to respond to the request. Do not include passwords or private game information that you do not want to share. Support correspondence is needed while the request is being handled; you may ask for its deletion, subject to applicable retention duties. Your email provider and any external site you open may handle connection or access information under their own policies. The game itself does not send telemetry to those services.

Children and changes

The game has no child profile, parent account or age-verification account. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions. We will update this page and its effective date when the app’s data practices change; material changes will also be described in release information. This policy covers Junqi Table, not the publisher’s other apps.
